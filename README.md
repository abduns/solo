# Solo

Project tracking for solo founders, on your Mac. Workspaces for your products, your client work and your own life;
projects, milestones and tasks; a Week page that drafts your changelog and client updates; a Calendar with your due
dates, milestones and dates like renewals; quick capture from any app; and bots that act as teammates.

Your data lives on your Mac. If you turn them on:

- **Sync** keeps Solo the same on your Macs through your own iCloud. There's no Solo server: your data goes only to
  your private iCloud database. Sync is part of Solo Pro (below).
- **Put Solo in Calendar** keeps a Solo calendar in Apple Calendar, so what's due reaches your iPhone and Watch.

## Download

**[Download the latest Solo for Mac](https://github.com/abduns/solo/releases/latest)**: open the DMG and drag Solo to
Applications.

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.
- Signed and notarized by Apple.
- Solo updates itself. It looks for updates when it opens and every few hours, downloads them in the background, and
  asks before restarting. You can turn this off in Settings → Preferences → Updates.

## Solo Pro

Everything is free except sync, which is part of Solo Pro: bought once, for up to 3 Macs. Buy it from Settings → Sync
→ Buy Solo Pro. Your license key comes by email: paste it in Settings → Sync and click Activate, on each Mac.
