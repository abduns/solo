# Solodun

Project tracking for solo founders, on your Mac. Workspaces for your products, your client work and your own life;
projects, milestones and tasks; a Week page that drafts your changelog and client updates; quick capture from any app;
and bots that act as teammates. Everything stays on your Mac.

## Download

**[Download the latest Solodun for Mac](https://github.com/abduns/solo/releases/latest)**: open the DMG and drag
Solodun to Applications.

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).
- Signed and notarized by Apple.
- Solodun updates itself. It looks for updates when it opens and every few hours, downloads them in the background,
  and asks before restarting. You can turn this off in Settings → Preferences → Updates.
